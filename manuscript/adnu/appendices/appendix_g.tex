\section{Safety Guideline Proposal to the PPA}\label{appendix-g.2-safety-guideline-proposal-to-the-ppa}

\textbf{PROPOSED SAFETY GUIDELINES AND TESTING PROTOCOLS}

For the Conduct of Electrical Fault Simulation during Data Collection
and Testing

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\Roman{enumi}.}
\item
  TITLE AND PROPONENTS
\end{enumerate}

The research project, titled \emph{TinyML Smart Plug for Complementary
Protection Against Progressive Fire-Inducing Electrical Faults in
Residential Circuits}, is presented by \textbf{Alex A. Alferez, Jett M.
Lumabi, and Francis M. Peña}, \textbf{4th-Year BS Electronics
Engineering students} from the Department of Electronics and Computer
Engineering at Ateneo de Naga University. Conducted under the advisement
of Dr. Referendo D. Soraino, the study is scheduled for its primary
development and validation phase from \textbf{January 2026 to May 2026}.

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\Roman{enumi}.}
\setcounter{enumi}{1}
\item
  OVERVIEW
\end{enumerate}

The study aims to develop an intelligent, ESP32-based smart plug capable
of detecting fire-inducing electrical faults that standard circuit
breakers often miss. These faults are Series Arc Faults, Silent
Sustained Overloads, and Contact Heating.

Since these faults are inherently dangerous, this proposal seeks the
assistance of trained personnel from the Physical Plant Administration
(PPA) that are skilled in safely handling electrical circuits to ensure
the safe and controlled execution of laboratory simulations required for
data collection and system validation, in the specified date, time and
location.

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\Roman{enumi}.}
\setcounter{enumi}{2}
\item
  RATIONALE
\end{enumerate}

In the Philippines and especially the Bicol region, the absence of GFCI
and AFCI devices leaves households vulnerable to electrical arcing and
loose connections. Additionally, standard breakers offer no proactive
overload warnings; they only trip after the wiring has already begun to
deteriorate due to thermal damages. This triggers a positive feedback
loop where wiring degradation causes more arcing and wire resistance,
until it eventually leads to a fire.

Validating a solution for these electrical faults requires generating
them in a controlled environment. However, creating actual arcs,
inducing overloads, and artificially degrading actual electrical sockets
involves significant risk including fire, shock, and equipment damage.
As undergraduate Electronics Engineering students, we recognize that
high-voltage applications fall primarily within the domain of Electrical
Engineering and we are not yet equipped with the experience to safely
handle these dangerous faults. To mitigate potential risks and ensure
the safety of us students and the laboratory equipment and facilities,
\textbf{all high-voltage procedures and fault simulations will be
conducted under the direct supervision and execution of authorized PPA
personnel}.

IV. OBJECTIVES

The objective of this proposal is to safely collect actual electrical
waveform data of hazardous faults to train and test the TinyML fault
detection system, specifically:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\item
  To simulate Sustained Overloads (10A to 15A) to test for overload, the
  capacity of the MCB, and the accuracy of the current sensor;
\item
  To induce Contact Heating (\textgreater70°C) on test terminals and/or
  degraded socket to validate thermal sensors;
\item
  To simulate Series Arc Faults using a controlled arc generator setup
  under PPA supervision; and
\item
  To establish a clear and safe protocol where PPA personnel manage live
  wires and fault simulations while students handle data logging.
\end{enumerate}

V. SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\item
  The request covers the period of January 2026 to May 2026.
\item
  PPA assistance is requested specifically for handling live fault
  generation, emergency switching, and setup inspection.
\item
  Testing is limited to single-phase 230V AC in a controlled laboratory
  environment
\item
  PPA staff are not expected to debug the ESP32 code or electronics,
  only to manage the AC mains supply and fault generation rigs for
  safety.
\end{enumerate}

VI. METHODOLOGY \& SAFETY PROTOCOLS

To ensure zero accidents, the following protocols will be strictly
observed throughout the testing period:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\item
  No high-voltage testing or fault simulation will commence without an
  authorized PPA staff member present.
\item
  The testing setup has a circuit breaker and the D315 workshop has a
  separate breaker to which the laboratory custodians and the PPA
  personnel have access to at all times.
\item
  All personnel inside the testing perimeter must wear rubber-soled
  safety shoes, insulated gloves, and safety glasses.
\item
  Students will handle data logging and software monitoring. PPA staff
  will handle live connections and fault triggers.
\end{enumerate}
