\chapter{Cost Breakdown}\label{app:i}

the original cost breakdown with additional component allowances for materials
used in the sensing, filtering, power, enclosure, wiring, and user-interface
subsystems. Online-listing prices should be rechecked before final submission
because supplier prices change.
