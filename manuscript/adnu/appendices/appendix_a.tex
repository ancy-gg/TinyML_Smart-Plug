\chapter{Sensor Calibration}\label{app:a}
This appendix contains the calibration datasets used to derive the
correction equations for the voltage, current, and temperature sensing
channels. These tables support the sensor-calibration procedures
described in Chapter 3 and the validation results discussed in Chapter
4.
